%% 3. Title + abstract
\begin{abstract}
We present a systematic survey of recent progress on real-time rendering
of dynamic 4D Gaussian Splatting (4DGS) on mobile GPUs
(Snapdragon 8 Elite / Adreno 830 + Vulkan 1.3). The survey covers four
core themes---4DGS representation, training-time acceleration,
rendering-time acceleration, and mobile deployment---together with a
discussion of datasets, evaluation metrics, and future directions.
The survey is the academic counterpart of a separate
engineering roadmap (the authors' production-track 4DGS
renderer); it emphasises breadth across the research
landscape rather than the engineering decisions of any one
project. We organise 180+ representative works published
between 2023 and the first half of 2026 into a nine-section
taxonomy and identify the dominant bottlenecks (storage,
bandwidth, compute) and the most promising research
directions for mobile deployment.
\end{abstract}

\section*{Authors' Short Biographies}
\textbf{Lianghao Zhang} is a graphics architect at Xiaomi Inc.,
Beijing, where he leads research on mobile real-time rendering and
GPU driver engineering. His current focus is the design and
prototyping of a Snapdragon 8 Elite / Adreno 830 + Vulkan 1.3
renderer for dynamic 4D Gaussian Splatting. He holds a Master's
degree in Computer Graphics from the Institute of Computing
Technology, Chinese Academy of Sciences, and has previously
contributed to Xiaomi's Vulkan driver, Mali toolchain integration,
and several internal OpenGL ES 3.2 optimisations. This survey is the academic counterpart of the
authors' production-track 4DGS renderer; the engineering
roadmap is documented separately and is not included here.
